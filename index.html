Questions

How is this different from a dashboard?+

You don't read Baysera, you talk to it. It tells you what matters, answers your questions, and handles the follow-through.

Does it really remember everything?+

Yes. What you tell it becomes part of how it understands your business. You can see, correct or delete anything it knows.

Will it act without asking me?+

Never. Baysera does the work up to the point of a decision. Nothing goes out until you approve it.

Can I trust its numbers?+

Every figure comes from a calculation on your own data and is checked before you hear it. The AI explains; it never invents numbers.

What does it connect to?+

The tools you already use, starting with booking platforms, spreadsheets and Instagram, with more on the way.

Is my data private?+

Your data is never used to train AI models, and Baysera only shares what each task needs.
