For service businesses

Your clients tell you everything. Just not out loud.

Studios, salons, clinics and spas run on returning clients. Baysera reads how each one books, comes and goes, tells you what's changing, and handles what comes next.

What it notices

01

Regulars drifting

A client who came every week now comes every other, long before they cancel.

02

Missed rebooking rhythm

Someone who always rebooks every six weeks is now at nine, with nothing in the calendar.

03

First visits that don't return

Which intro offers, times or staff turn a first visit into a second one, and which don't.

04

Demand by the hour

Slots with waitlists next to slots that sit half-empty, week after week.

05

No-shows and late cancels

Patterns by time, service and client, so you can fix the cause, not just the empty spot.

06

Loyalty to people

Clients who follow one instructor or stylist, and who's at risk when that person's hours change.

In practice

Illustrative examples

Fitness studio

Baysera notices

Early-morning regulars started skipping after the 6:30 class moved to 7:00.

Baysera does

Drafts a schedule change, checks it with the instructor, and publishes it once you approve.

Hair salon

Baysera notices

Colour clients are stretching past their usual rebooking time, mostly those who don't book at checkout.

Baysera does

Prepares a rebook-at-checkout routine for the front desk and follows up on how it's working.

Physio clinic

Baysera notices

Patients often drop off after their third session, before the treatment plan ends.

Baysera does

Suggests booking the full plan up front and sets up the change in your scheduling, with your sign-off.

Day spa

Baysera notices

Weekday afternoons sit quiet while weekend slots carry a waitlist.

Baysera does

Proposes a staffing shift and a weekday package, then tracks whether bookings move.
